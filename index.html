About

My name is Alfilla Deo Ramadanu, an IT student based in Yogyakarta. Beyond writing clean code and structuring applications, I channel my creativity into music and open roads.

Whether I'm laying down tracks on electronic drums and guitar, or cruising the scenic routes around Jogja on two wheels, I believe balance fuels better problem-solving in software development.

Open for tech collaborations, musical jams, and creative discussions.

My Art

The Sound of Lines & Ink

Portraiture & Musical Influence

Art has always been an extension of the sound I carry inside. Back in junior high school, that passion translated into drawing a portrait of Kurt Cobain—driven by a deep connection to his music, raw energy, and counter-culture spirit.

What started as capturing an idol on paper evolved into a lifelong appreciation for visual expression. Whether I'm writing clean logic in an IDE or sketching monochrome portraits, every stroke is fueled by the same rhythm and authenticity.
